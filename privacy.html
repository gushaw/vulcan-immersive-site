Privacy Policy for Vulcan Immersive

Last updated: November 2025

Vulcan Immersive is a personal and experimental virtual reality application designed to allow users to view and explore their Pinterest boards in an immersive environment.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Pinterest account, we may access:

  • Your public Pinterest profile information
  • Your boards and pins
  • Basic metadata related to your Pinterest content

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment details, or private messages.

2. How Information Is Used

We use this information only to:

  • Display your Pinterest boards and pins inside the VR environment
  • Improve the visual and interaction experience of the app

We do not sell, share, or redistribute your personal data or Pinterest content.

3. Data Storage

The app does not permanently store or archive Pinterest content on external servers. Board and pin data may be cached locally or temporarily for performance purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

This app uses the Pinterest API to retrieve authorized content and is subject to Pinterest’s own privacy and data use policies.

5. User Control

You can revoke the app’s access to your Pinterest account at any time through your Pinterest account settings.
